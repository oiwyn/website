Selembar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Selembar is a one-note app made by Owynn. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Selembar doesn't collect any data about you.

Your notes stay on your device

Your note, archived notes and settings (such as your chosen font) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to the developer, to a server or to anyone else. Selembar has no account or sign-in.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the note pinned to your Lock Screen as a Live Activity, read your note from the same on-device storage.

If you delete Selembar, its notes are deleted with it. Your notes may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

No tracking or analytics

  • Selembar does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics or location.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not connect to the internet, apart from in-app purchases described below.

Tips (in-app purchases)

You can optionally buy the developer a coffee from the About page. These purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or any other personal information; Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Links to websites

The About page links to outside websites, which open in Safari. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Selembar collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
